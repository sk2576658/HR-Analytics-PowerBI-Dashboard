# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI** to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends across different employee and organizational dimensions.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![HR Analytics Dashboard](Screenshot/HR%20Analytics%20Dashboard.png)

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition and workforce trends using Power BI.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of employee attrition across:

- Age Groups
- Education Fields
- Salary Slabs
- Job Roles
- Job Satisfaction Levels
- Years at Company
- Departments

Interactive slicers allow users to filter the analysis by **Gender, Department, and Marital Status**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition is an important HR metric that can help organizations understand workforce turnover and identify patterns in employee exits.

The objective of this project is to create an interactive dashboard that helps analyze:

- Overall employee count
- Attrition count
- Attrition rate
- Average employee age
- Average monthly salary
- Average years at company
- Department-wise attrition
- Age-wise attrition
- Salary-wise attrition
- Education-wise attrition
- Job role and satisfaction patterns
- Tenure-wise attrition

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard includes the following KPI cards:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Employee Count | Total number of employees |
| Attrition Rate | Percentage of employees who left |
| Attrition Count | Number of employees who left |
| Average Age | Average age of employees |
| Average Monthly Salary | Average monthly income |
| Average Years | Average years employees have spent at the company |

All KPI values are dynamic and respond to the selected filters.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Attrition by Education

**Visual:** Donut Chart

Shows the distribution of employee attrition across different education fields.

### 2. Attrition by Age Group

**Visual:** Clustered Column Chart

Compares employee attrition across different age groups.

### 3. Attrition by Salary

**Visual:** Horizontal Bar Chart

Shows employee attrition across different salary slabs.

### 4. Job Role vs Job Satisfaction Level

**Visual:** Matrix

Shows the distribution of job satisfaction levels across different job roles.

### 5. Attrition by Years at Company

**Visual:** Line Chart

Analyzes employee attrition across different years of experience at the company.

### 6. Attrition by Department

**Visual:** Column Chart

Compares the number of employees who left across different departments.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes the following slicers:

- **Gender**
- **Department**
- **Marital Status**

These filters dynamically update the KPI cards and visualizations.
